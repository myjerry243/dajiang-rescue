About Us

A Professional Water Rescue Equipment Manufacturer

Founded in September 2018 and located in Shenzhen, China, the company is a specialist manufacturer of water rescue equipment — a high-tech enterprise integrating R&D, design, production and sales.

Our product range covers water surface rescue stretchers, surface rescue flying wings, surface rescue robots, flying rescue boats, flying life buoys, remote-controlled life buoys, underwater search & rescue robots, unmanned survey boats, fire reconnaissance robots and firefighting robots.

Guided by the spirit of "integrity first, innovation at heart", we are committed to high efficiency, high quality and customer satisfaction.
